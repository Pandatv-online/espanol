Learn Spanish A1–C2: all topics

Free Spanish grammar lessons and vocabulary with examples for levels A1–C2.

  • A1 — Beginner

    You understand and use basic phrases: introduce yourself, ask how someone is, handle numbers and prices.

  • A2 — Elementary

    You handle everyday situations: shopping, directions, talking about yourself and last weekend.

  • B1 — Intermediate

    You get by when travelling, describe experiences and plans, and explain your opinion.

  • B2 — Upper intermediate

    You talk with native speakers on many topics with ease, follow the news and argue your point.

  • C1 — Advanced

    You understand long, complex texts and implied meaning, and speak fluently with precise wording.

  • C2 — Proficient

    You understand virtually everything and express fine shades of meaning like an educated native speaker.

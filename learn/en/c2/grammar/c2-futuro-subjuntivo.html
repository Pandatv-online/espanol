Futuro de subjuntivo and archaic forms

Hablare, tuviere, fuere: the form of laws, sayings and solemn formulas — and what replaces it today

Futuro de subjuntivo

Formation

How to form it

Like the Imperfecto de subjuntivo, it comes from the ellos form of the Indefinido: drop -ron and add -re, -res, -re, -remos, -reis, -ren. hablaron → hablare, tuvieron → tuviere, fueron → fuere.

The nosotros form has an accent: habláremos, tuviéremos. The compound form is hubiere + participle: hubiere cometido.

It is easy to confuse with the Imperfecto de subjuntivo: tuviera (imperfecto) vs tuviere (futuro). The difference is one letter: -ra vs -re.

One letter, three forms

hablare — futuro de subjuntivo · hablara — imperfecto de subjuntivo · hablaré — the ordinary future (stress on the end).

The plural works the same way: habláremos — habláramos — hablaremos.

Legal texts

Where you find it

This form disappeared from everyday speech centuries ago. It survives in legal and administrative texts (especially older laws and regulations), in sayings and in solemn formulas. You need to recognise it; use it yourself only for stylistic effect.

It means a hypothetical future: “if at any time someone…”. Today it is replaced by the present subjunctive (after que, quien, cuando) or the present indicative (after si).

Typical patterns in laws

Whoever does…

the person the rule applies to

el que · quien · los que · toda persona que

Quien causare daños a bienes ajenos estará obligado a repararlos.

Whoever causes damage to another’s property shall be obliged to make it good.

Condition

if, unless, in case

si · salvo que · en caso de que

Si el arrendatario no abonare la renta, el contrato quedará resuelto.

Should the tenant fail to pay the rent, the contract shall be terminated.

Time

when, while, until

cuando · mientras · hasta que

Mientras no se dictare sentencia firme, el acusado se presumirá inocente.

Until a final judgment is handed down, the accused shall be presumed innocent.

Compound form

an action completed by then

hubiere + participio

Los que hubieren participado en el delito responderán solidariamente.

All those who have taken part in the offence shall be jointly liable.

Other marks of legal style

Haber de + infinitive expresses obligation in regulations: Los aspirantes habrán de acreditar su experiencia. Formulas: so pena de (“on pain of”), por la presente (“hereby”), el susodicho (“the aforementioned”).

Bookish and legal style

Set phrases

Sayings and solemn formulas

In a handful of sayings and formulas the futuro de subjuntivo is frozen for good. They are not rebuilt; people say them as they have come down — sea quien fuere. Modern versions with the present subjunctive exist alongside them.

The same form is heard in oaths (“and should you fail to do so…”) and in the legal phrase a que hubiere lugar — “as may be appropriate”.

What replaces it today

After que, quien, el que, cuando, donde use the present subjunctive; after si the present indicative; hubiere + participle → haya + participle.

Don’t mix them up

The present subjunctive is impossible after si: not “si alguien altere” but si alguien altera. And don’t slip the -re form into ordinary speech: cuando llegare in a letter to a friend sounds like a parody of an old law.

Other old-fashioned features

A pronoun attached after a conjugated verb: díjole = le dijo, hallábase = se hallaba, sentóse = se sentó. Found in old literature and deliberately bookish style; today the pronoun goes before the verb.

Examples

Laws and regulations

  • El que infringiere esta norma será sancionado.Whoever breaks this rule shall be penalised.
  • Si alguien alterare el orden, será expulsado.Should anyone disturb the peace, they shall be removed.
  • Quien no cumpliere lo dispuesto en este artículo incurrirá en falta grave.Anyone who fails to comply with this article will commit a serious offence.
  • El que presenciare un delito está obligado a ponerlo en conocimiento del juez.Anyone who witnesses an offence is obliged to report it to the judge.
  • Si hubiere más de un heredero, la herencia se dividirá a partes iguales.Should there be more than one heir, the estate shall be divided equally.
  • Si el deudor no pagare en el plazo fijado, se procederá al embargo.Should the debtor fail to pay by the set deadline, his assets shall be seized.
  • Quien hallare un objeto perdido deberá entregarlo en el ayuntamiento.Whoever finds a lost item must hand it in at the town hall.
  • Los candidatos habrán de presentar la solicitud antes del día 5.Candidates shall submit their applications before the 5th.
  • Queda prohibido fumar en el recinto, so pena de multa.Smoking on the premises is prohibited, on pain of a fine.

Sayings and formulas

  • Adonde fueres, haz lo que vieres.When in Rome, do as the Romans do.
  • Si así no lo hiciereis, que Dios y la Patria os lo demanden.Should you fail to do so, may God and the Nation hold you to account.
  • Sea lo que fuere, mañana lo sabremos.Whatever it may be, we’ll know tomorrow.
  • Venga lo que viniere, estaremos juntos.Come what may, we’ll be together.
  • Fuere como fuere, el plazo ya ha vencido.Be that as it may, the deadline has already passed.
  • Se le impondrán las sanciones a que hubiere lugar.The appropriate penalties shall be imposed on him.
  • Lo que aquí se dijere quedará entre estas paredes.Whatever may be said here shall stay within these walls.

Archaic and modern

  • El que infrinja esta norma será sancionado.Whoever breaks this rule will be penalised (modern form).
  • Si alguien altera el orden, será expulsado.If anyone disturbs the peace, they will be removed (modern form).
  • Adonde vayas, haz lo que veas.Wherever you go, do as you see others do (modern form of the saying).
  • Los que hubieren abonado la matrícula podrán solicitar su devolución.Those who have paid the enrolment fee may request a refund.
  • Los que hayan abonado la matrícula podrán solicitar su devolución.Those who have paid the enrolment fee may request a refund (modern form).
  • Cuando llegare el momento, sabremos qué hacer.When the time comes, we shall know what to do.
  • Cuando llegue el momento, sabremos qué hacer.When the time comes, we’ll know what to do (modern form).
  • Si tuviere alguna duda, consulte al personal de sala.Should you have any questions, please ask the staff.
  • Si tiene alguna duda, consulte al personal de sala.If you have any questions, please ask the staff (modern form).

Other old-fashioned features

  • Hallábase el caballero en su castillo cuando llegó la noticia.The knight was in his castle when the news arrived.
  • Díjole entonces el anciano que nada temiese.Then the old man told him to fear nothing.
  • Sentóse la dama junto a la ventana y guardó silencio.The lady sat down by the window and fell silent.

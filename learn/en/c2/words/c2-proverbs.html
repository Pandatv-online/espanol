Proverbs and sayings

Proverbs and sayings: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level C2.

Más vale tarde que nunca
Better late than never

Por fin te has sacado el carné. ¡Más vale tarde que nunca!

You've finally got your licence. Better late than never!

A caballo regalado no le mires el diente
Don't look a gift horse in the mouth

El móvil es viejo, pero a caballo regalado no le mires el diente.

The phone is old, but don't look a gift horse in the mouth.

Más vale pájaro en mano que ciento volando
A bird in the hand is worth two in the bush

Acepta la oferta: más vale pájaro en mano que ciento volando.

Take the offer: a bird in the hand is worth two in the bush.

En casa del herrero, cuchillo de palo
The cobbler's children go barefoot

Es informático y su ordenador no funciona: en casa del herrero, cuchillo de palo.

He works in IT and his own computer doesn't work: the cobbler's children go barefoot.

No hay mal que por bien no venga
Every cloud has a silver lining

Perdí el tren y conocí a mi mujer: no hay mal que por bien no venga.

I missed the train and met my wife: every cloud has a silver lining.

Dime con quién andas y te diré quién eres
A man is known by the company he keeps
Perro ladrador, poco mordedor
His bark is worse than his bite

No te preocupes por sus amenazas: perro ladrador, poco mordedor.

Don't worry about his threats: his bark is worse than his bite.

Quien calla, otorga
Silence gives consent

¿Nadie se opone? Pues quien calla, otorga: queda aprobado.

Nobody objects? Silence gives consent: it’s approved.

Ojos que no ven, corazón que no siente
What you don't know can't hurt you

No le cuentes lo del coche: ojos que no ven, corazón que no siente.

Don't tell him about the car: what he doesn't know can't hurt him.

Quien mucho abarca, poco aprieta
Don't bite off more than you can chew

Deja algún proyecto: quien mucho abarca, poco aprieta.

Drop one of your projects: don't bite off more than you can chew.

A quien madruga, Dios le ayuda
The early bird catches the worm

Llegué el primero y conseguí el mejor sitio: a quien madruga, Dios le ayuda.

I got there first and got the best spot: the early bird catches the worm.

Del dicho al hecho hay mucho trecho
Easier said than done

Prometió cambiar, pero del dicho al hecho hay mucho trecho.

He promised to change, but that's easier said than done.

Cría cuervos y te sacarán los ojos
To nurture a viper in your bosom
Agua pasada no mueve molino
It's water under the bridge

Olvida aquella discusión: agua pasada no mueve molino.

Forget that argument: it's water under the bridge.

To each their own

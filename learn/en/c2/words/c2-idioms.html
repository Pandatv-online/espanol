C2 · Vocabulary

Idioms and set phrases

Idioms and set phrases: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level C2.

tomar el pelo
to pull someone's leg

¿Me estás tomando el pelo?

Are you pulling my leg?

estar en las nubes
to have one's head in the clouds

Perdona, estaba en las nubes.

Sorry, I was miles away.

meter la pata
to put one's foot in it

Metí la pata al preguntarle por su ex.

I put my foot in it by asking about his ex.

no tener pelos en la lengua
not to mince one's words

Mi abuela no tiene pelos en la lengua.

My grandmother doesn't mince her words.

costar un ojo de la cara
to cost an arm and a leg

El piso nos costó un ojo de la cara.

The flat cost us an arm and a leg.

ser pan comido
to be a piece of cake

El examen fue pan comido.

The exam was a piece of cake.

dar gato por liebre
to rip someone off, to palm something off on someone

En esa tienda te dan gato por liebre.

That shop will rip you off.

estar entre la espada y la pared
to be between a rock and a hard place

Estoy entre la espada y la pared: si acepto, pierdo a mis clientes.

I'm between a rock and a hard place: if I accept, I lose my clients.

tirar la toalla
to throw in the towel

No tires la toalla ahora que falta tan poco.

Don't throw in the towel now that you're so close.

ponerse las pilas
to get one's act together

Si quieres aprobar, ponte las pilas.

If you want to pass, get your act together.

hablar por los codos
to talk someone's ear off

Mi vecina habla por los codos.

My neighbour never stops talking.

hacer la vista gorda
to turn a blind eye

El jefe hace la vista gorda con los retrasos.

The boss turns a blind eye to people arriving late.

a buenas horas, mangas verdes
too little, too late

¿Ahora me pides perdón? A buenas horas, mangas verdes.

You're apologising now? Too little, too late.

ser uña y carne
to be joined at the hip, to be as thick as thieves

Esas dos son uña y carne.

Those two are joined at the hip.

buscarle tres pies al gato
to overcomplicate things, to split hairs

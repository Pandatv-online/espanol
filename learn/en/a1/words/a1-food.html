A1 · Vocabulary

Food and drink

Food and drink: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level A1.

el agua
water

Un vaso de agua, por favor.

A glass of water, please.

el pan
bread

Compro pan cada día.

I buy bread every day.

la leche
milk

¿Tomas café con leche?

Do you have coffee with milk?

el café
coffee
el zumo
juice (Latin America: el jugo)

Un zumo de naranja, por favor.

An orange juice, please.

la carne
meat

No como carne.

I don't eat meat.

el pescado
fish (food)

Hoy cenamos pescado.

We're having fish for dinner today.

el pollo
chicken
el huevo
egg

Desayuno dos huevos.

I have two eggs for breakfast.

el queso
cheese
la manzana
apple

Esta manzana está muy buena.

This apple is very good.

las verduras
vegetables
el arroz
rice

La paella se hace con arroz.

Paella is made with rice.

comer
to eat; to have lunch

Comemos a las dos.

We have lunch at two.

beber
to drink

Health

Health: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level A2.

la salud
health

Fumar es malo para la salud.

Smoking is bad for your health.

sano/a
healthy

Intento comer sano.

I try to eat healthily.

enfermo/a
ill, sick

Hoy no voy a clase, estoy enfermo.

I'm not going to class today, I'm ill.

el dolor
pain, ache
tener dolor de cabeza
to have a headache

Tengo dolor de cabeza desde ayer.

I've had a headache since yesterday.

la fiebre
fever, temperature

El niño tiene fiebre.

The child has a temperature.

el resfriado
cold (illness)
la gripe
flu

Estuve una semana con gripe.

I had the flu for a week.

la tos
cough
la cita
appointment

Quiero pedir cita con el médico.

I'd like to make an appointment with the doctor.

la receta
prescription; recipe

pill, tablet

Tómate una pastilla después de comer.

Take a pill after lunch.

el jarabe
syrup (medicine)
las urgencias
A&E, emergency room

Lo llevaron a urgencias.

They took him to A&E.

descansar
to rest

Tienes que descansar unos días.

You need to rest for a few days.

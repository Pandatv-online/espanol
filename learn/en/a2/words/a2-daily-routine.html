Daily routine

Daily routine: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level A2.

despertarse
to wake up

Me despierto a las siete.

I wake up at seven.

levantarse
to get up

Los domingos me levanto tarde.

On Sundays I get up late.

ducharse
to have a shower

Me ducho por la noche.

I shower in the evening.

lavarse los dientes
to brush your teeth
vestirse
to get dressed

Me visto rápido y salgo.

I get dressed quickly and go out.

desayunar
to have breakfast

Desayuno café con tostadas.

I have coffee and toast for breakfast.

salir de casa
to leave home

Salgo de casa a las ocho.

I leave home at eight.

volver a casa
to come back home
cenar
to have dinner

En España cenamos a las diez.

In Spain we have dinner at ten.

acostarse
to go to bed

Me acuesto a medianoche.

I go to bed at midnight.

En verano duermo la siesta.

In summer I have a nap after lunch.

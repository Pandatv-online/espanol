B1 · Vocabulary

Opinions and debate

Opinions and debate: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level B1.

la opinión
opinion

Respeto tu opinión, pero no la comparto.

I respect your opinion, but I don't share it.

en mi opinión
in my opinion

En mi opinión, es demasiado caro.

In my opinion, it's too expensive.

creo que
I think (that)

Creo que tienes razón.

I think you're right.

el punto de vista
point of view
estar de acuerdo
to agree

No estoy de acuerdo contigo.

I don't agree with you.

estar en contra de
to be against

Estoy en contra de las corridas de toros.

I'm against bullfighting.

tener razón
to be right

Al final tenías razón.

You were right in the end.

el debate
debate
convencer
to convince

No me convence la idea.

I'm not convinced by the idea.

la ventaja
advantage

Vivir en el centro tiene muchas ventajas.

Living in the centre has many advantages.

el inconveniente
drawback, disadvantage
además
besides, moreover

Es barato y, además, está cerca.

It's cheap and, besides, it's close.

sin embargo
however

Es un buen plan; sin embargo, es caro.

It's a good plan; however, it's expensive.

por un lado… por otro lado
on the one hand… on the other hand

Por un lado es cómodo; por otro lado, es caro.

On the one hand it's convenient; on the other, it's expensive.

depende
it depends

¿Vienes mañana? — Depende del tiempo.

Are you coming tomorrow? — It depends on the weather.

B2 · Vocabulary

Bureaucracy and paperwork

Bureaucracy and paperwork: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level B2.

el trámite
formality, procedure

Los trámites para abrir una empresa son largos.

The paperwork to set up a company takes a long time.

la solicitud
application

Rellene la solicitud con letra clara.

Fill in the application legibly.

rellenar un formulario
to fill in a form

Hay que rellenar un formulario en la web.

You have to fill in a form on the website.

la cita previa
appointment (booked in advance)

Sin cita previa no te atienden.

They won't see you without an appointment.

el plazo
deadline, time limit

El plazo termina el 30 de junio.

The deadline is 30 June.

la ventanilla
counter, service window

Pase a la ventanilla número tres.

Please go to window number three.

el certificado
certificate

Necesito un certificado de empadronamiento.

I need a certificate of registration at my address.

empadronarse
to register as a resident (at the town hall)

Primero tienes que empadronarte en el ayuntamiento.

First you have to register at the town hall.

el permiso de residencia
residence permit

Estoy esperando el permiso de residencia.

I'm waiting for my residence permit.

la fotocopia compulsada
certified copy
firmar
to sign

Firme aquí, por favor.

Sign here, please.

el sello
stamp, seal

Sin el sello, el documento no es válido.

Without the stamp the document isn't valid.

caducado/a
expired

Tu pasaporte está caducado.

Your passport has expired.

el funcionario / la funcionaria
civil servant, official
la Seguridad Social
Social Security

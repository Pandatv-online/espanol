Medicine and health

Medicine and health: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level B2.

el diagnóstico
diagnosis

El diagnóstico fue una simple gripe.

The diagnosis was just the flu.

el síntoma
symptom

¿Qué síntomas tiene?

What symptoms do you have?

el historial médico
medical records

La doctora revisó mi historial médico.

The doctor looked through my medical records.

el tratamiento
treatment

El tratamiento dura dos semanas.

The treatment lasts two weeks.

los efectos secundarios
side effects

Este medicamento puede tener efectos secundarios.

This medicine may have side effects.

la cirugía
surgery (the field)
operar
to operate (on)

Lo operaron de la rodilla.

He had knee surgery.

el análisis de sangre
blood test

Tengo que hacerme un análisis de sangre en ayunas.

I need to have a blood test on an empty stomach.

la baja médica
sick leave

Está de baja médica desde el lunes.

He's been on sick leave since Monday.

la anestesia
anaesthetic, anaesthesia

Le pusieron anestesia local.

He was given a local anaesthetic.

la vacuna
vaccine

La vacuna de la gripe es gratuita para los mayores.

The flu vaccine is free for older people.

contagioso/a
contagious

No vengas, todavía eres contagioso.

Don't come, you're still contagious.

crónico/a
chronic
recuperarse
to recover

Se está recuperando poco a poco.

She's recovering little by little.

el/la especialista
specialist

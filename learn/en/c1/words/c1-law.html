C1 · Vocabulary

Law and justice

Law and justice: 15 Spanish words with translations and examples. Level C1.

el juicio
trial

El juicio empieza la semana que viene.

The trial starts next week.

el juez / la jueza
judge

La jueza lo absolvió.

The judge acquitted him.

el/la fiscal
public prosecutor
el acusado / la acusada
the accused, defendant

El acusado se negó a declarar.

The defendant refused to testify.

la sentencia
sentence, ruling

La sentencia se conocerá el lunes.

The ruling will be announced on Monday.

el delito
crime, offence

Conducir sin carné es un delito.

Driving without a licence is a criminal offence.

denunciar
to report (to the police)

Denunció el robo en comisaría.

She reported the theft at the police station.

el/la testigo
witness

Hubo tres testigos del accidente.

There were three witnesses to the accident.

la prueba
evidence, proof

No hay pruebas suficientes contra él.

There isn't enough evidence against him.

culpable
guilty

El jurado lo declaró culpable.

The jury found him guilty.

la presunción de inocencia
presumption of innocence
recurrir
to appeal

La defensa va a recurrir la sentencia.

The defence is going to appeal the ruling.

la multa
fine

Me pusieron una multa por aparcar mal.

I got a fine for parking illegally.

la impunidad
impunity
el derecho
law (as a subject); right

Estudió Derecho en Salamanca.

She studied law in Salamanca.
